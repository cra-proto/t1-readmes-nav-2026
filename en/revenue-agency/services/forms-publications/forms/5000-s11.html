5000-S11 Schedule 11 - Federal Tuition Amount and Canada Training Credit (for all except QC and non-residents)

You may be looking for: All personal income tax packages

Instructions for downloading fillable PDFs

To complete a fillable form, you must download the accessible fillable PDF to your computer. Use Acrobat Reader 10 or later to open the file. Do not click the link to open it in your web browser.

  1. Download and save the PDF to your computer
  2. Open the downloaded PDF in Acrobat Reader 10 or later

If you were a student, use Schedule 11 to calculate federal tuition amounts and the Canada training credit, including amounts available to transfer or carry forward.

In previous years, this schedule was used to calculate additional similar amounts:

  • Prior to 2020: tuition, education, and textbook amounts only
  • From 2020 to 2024: tuition, education, and textbook amounts and Canada training credit
